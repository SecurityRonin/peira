# Chesterton's Fence

**peira code:** `CHESTERTON` · **Modern — analytic and scientific method** · **Catalogued** — a reading, owns no gate

**Failure mode:** removing something without recovering why it was put there

**What it does:** a `supersedes` edge requires a recorded reason the prior existed

**Worked example:** Before the fence comes down, the reason it went up must be stated — not because the reason is necessarily good, but because not knowing it is not an argument.

**Sources:**
- G. K. Chesterton, The Thing (1929), ch. 4 "The Drift from Domesticity"
