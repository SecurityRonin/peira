# The Three Marks of a Valid Reason 因三相

<!-- Generated from peira_lens::CATALOG by cli/examples/gen_site.rs. Edit the lens in lens/src/lib.rs and regenerate. -->

**peira code:** `THREE-MARKS` · **Buddhist — Madhyamaka and Dignāgan logic** · **Enforced** — owns a gate

**Failure mode:** a reason that also holds where the claim is false, so it proves nothing while looking like proof

**What it does:** support common to every side of a live contest carries no weight for any of them

**Enforced gates:** `PEIR-REASON-UNDIAGNOSTIC`, `PEIR-CONTRARY-CASES-UNSURVEYED`, `PEIR-CONFIRMING-CASE-UNDECLARED`

**Worked example:** An Amcache InventoryApplicationFile entry supports "the user ran it" and "the appraiser catalogued it, unrun" equally, because the appraiser writes one either way. A case resting on it alone has argued for neither side — 共不定, cell 1 of the 九句因. One Prefetch .pf with a run counter decides the contest, and passes the gate.
