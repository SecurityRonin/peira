# Privacy

elenchus is a command-line program that reads and writes files on your machine.

- **No network access.** The binary makes no outbound connections. There is no
  telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no update check.
- **No data leaves your machine.** Vault content, packets and digests are read from and
  written to paths you name.
- **No accounts.** There is nothing to sign in to.

Reviewer identities you write into `graded_by` are ordinary text in your own files,
under your control, and are never transmitted anywhere.
