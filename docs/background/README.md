# Background

The three documents that motivated peira, kept verbatim as they were written.
They are **source material, not specifications** — where peira diverges from
them, peira is the current state and these are the record of what was intended.

| Document | What it contributes |
|---|---|
| [`organization-method-ogs.md`](organization-method-ogs.md) | **OGS (Open · Governed · Sealed)** — where knowledge *lives*. Trust zones, Johnny.Decimal areas `00-59`, typed frontmatter, the one-way privilege membrane. peira claims areas `60-99` and enforces the membrane as a lint. |
| [`vibe-hacking-village-vibe-research-core-2026-08-09.md`](vibe-hacking-village-vibe-research-core-2026-08-09.md) | **Vibe Research** — how a claim *earns its status*. | [`feature-comparison-matrix.md`](feature-comparison-matrix.md) | **murdock** — a terminal-native legal AI for barristers, and the consumer that motivated the whole thing. |

## On murdock

**murdock is a separate product, not part of peira.** peira is the domain-neutral
examination engine; murdock is a jurisdiction-specific application that would
consume it, alongside a `peira-legal` domain pack. Keeping them apart is what
lets the lens catalogue stay universal — a meta-test enforces that it draws on
five or more traditions and holds only domain-neutral lenses.

The name `murdock` has not had a clearance pass and should get one before it has
any code: it reads as Matt Murdock (Daredevil), which is Marvel/Disney IP, and a
legal tool sold to lawyers is the worst possible audience for that kind of
exposure.

## What was missing

None of the three says what *review actually is*. Vibe Research names the states
and says review happens; it never says what happens during it. peira is that
middle layer.
